DuoScore Support

Write to support@duoscore.app. Inside the app, Settings → Support → Email support writes the message for you, with your app version and device.

Common questions

How does the free trial work?

Start free trial gives you every feature for 7 days. After that, DuoScore stays a player until you buy DuoScore Unlock, €9.99 once. The Terms of Use have the details.

I bought DuoScore Unlock on another device. How do I get it here?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then Settings → Purchase → Restore purchases.

Which files does DuoScore open?

Guitar Pro files (.gp, .gpx, .gp5, .gp4, .gp3) and more, from the Files app or shared from other apps. It saves songs as .gp files, and exports PDF and MIDI.

Can I move drums to and from other music apps?

Yes. Drum tracks export to and import from MIDI with custom drum mapping. Right now it comes prebuilt with the Modern & Massive 2 map alongside General MIDI, and future updates will add more presets plus custom mapping directly in the app.

Which devices does DuoScore work on?

Every iPhone and iPad that runs iOS 27. On iPhone it edits held upright or sideways; on iPad, in three columns. On the iPhone Duo, open, it's a full editor; closed, the outer screen writes and plays too, and playback pauses when you fold or unfold it.

How do I see the tour again?

Settings → Guide replays the one-minute tour. It writes another "My first riff" into your Library, and the one from before stays.
